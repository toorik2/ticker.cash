A notarized BCH/USD price feed on Bitcoin Cash. Thirteen notaries, each pinned to one exchange, co-run a TLS session with it, verify its certificate, and sign the price they saw. A covenant takes the lower median of at least seven of those signatures and writes it on chain. Once a minute, in one transaction.

Nobody in the system chooses a number. Notaries sign only what they verified; anyone can open the sessions and anyone can post the update. The trust root is 7 of 13 verifiers, and the covenant reads nothing but its own input and outputs. No admin keys, no timestamps, no clock.

Today ticker.cash operates all thirteen rows, so the honest description is one operator who cryptographically verified each exchange. A row is one small binary and one exchange endpoint, built to be handed to independent operators; the stats page names the operator of every row.

Read the price on chain. Spend a Ticker NFT in your transaction and decode its 12-byte commitment: the Unix minute and the price. Integration guide, covenant walkthrough, and how to run a notary in the docs.
